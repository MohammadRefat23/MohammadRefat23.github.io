## Overview

The Jhelum stellar stream is a tidal stream in the Milky Way halo produced by the disruption of a smaller stellar system. I contributed to identifying candidate members by combining APOGEE-2 spectroscopy with Gaia astrometry as part of the Sloan Digital Sky Survey FAST collaboration.

## Scientific Motivation

Stellar streams preserve the formation history of the Milky Way. By combining spectroscopy and astrometry, researchers can identify stream members and study their chemistry and dynamics to better understand Galactic evolution.

## Research Questions

- Which APOGEE-2 stars belong to the Jhelum stream?
- How can Gaia astrometry improve membership selection?
- What do chemistry and kinematics reveal about the stream's origin?
