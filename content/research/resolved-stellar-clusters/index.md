## Overview

This ongoing project at the American Museum of Natural History focuses on estimating membership in resolved stellar clusters from astrometric measurements. I am revising a Python-based probabilistic workflow that distinguishes cluster populations from field stars while accounting for reported measurement uncertainties.

## My Contributions

- Revising probabilistic calculations used to estimate stellar-cluster membership.
- Incorporating proper-motion measurement uncertainties into the analysis.
- Evaluating how the field-star model interacts with one or more candidate cluster components.
- Reviewing model assumptions and probability outputs with diagnostic calculations and visualizations.

## Methods

The workflow uses Python for data handling and numerical analysis, with probabilistic component modeling of stellar proper motions. Current work includes examining how measurement uncertainties and constraints on the field-star distribution affect membership estimates.

## Current Status

The project is in progress. I am checking the statistical formulation and validating the probability calculations; final model validation and scientific conclusions are not yet reported here.
